    Put it on your home screen: in Safari tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". It opens like an app.

    Adding drivers to Contacts: tap "Add drivers to Contacts", then on the share sheet pick Save to Files. Open the Files app, tap the saved file, and tap Add All Contacts. Sending it to yourself in Messages or Mail works the same way.

    No signal? Once this app has opened with data one time, it opens without it. Calling and texting only need cell service, and GPS works with no data. The driver needs data to open the map link, so texting your campsite or a landmark is the safest bet.

    Calls and texts use your phone's normal dialer and Messages. Nothing here is sent anywhere else.
